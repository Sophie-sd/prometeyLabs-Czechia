This Policy is part of the Offer. Mandatory consumer rights cannot be excluded.

2. When a refund is available

Services are custom development.

A full or partial refund applies in particular where:

  1. there is a complete absence of results due to the Contractor’s fault within the deadline (after a reasonable cure);
  2. the result materially fails the TS and defects are not fixed;
  3. a consumer has a statutory withdrawal or defect right (reduction / refund).

Partial refunds may apply for partial non-performance by the Contractor, consumer defect rights, or written agreement. An absolute ban on partial refunds does not apply to consumers where it would conflict with law.

Force majeure (Offer Art. 9) extends deadlines; it does not automatically refund a deposit.

3. When generally not

Without prejudice to consumer rights, we generally do not refund where:

  1. the work matches the TS and dissatisfaction is only subjective (“I don’t like it”, “I changed my mind”);
  2. delay was caused by the Client (materials / feedback > 14 days) — for B2B the deposit may cover idle time;
  3. the Contract ended for serious toxic Client behaviour — subject to mandatory consumer rights;
  4. after 100% payment and handover with no defect or legal ground.

4. Request

Within 14 calendar days of the grounds in Article 2 (statutory consumer defect periods prevail). Email: info@prometeylabs.cz — name, invoice/TS, reasons, evidence, consumer or trader status.

5. Assessment and payout

Assessment within 10 business days. If approved, refund via the same channel within 14 business days. The amount may be reduced by non-refundable Comgate / GoPay fees where consumer law allows.

VAT: not registered for CZ/EU VAT.

6. Consumers

Rights under Directive 2011/83/EU and in Czechia Sections 1829 / 1837 of the Civil Code remain (withdrawal; exceptions for early performance with consent and made-to-order work) — see Offer Article 11. Defect rights remain.

ADR: ČOI (www.coi.cz, adr.coi.cz) · ODR (ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr).

B2B: Ukrainian law per Offer Article 10. Consumer: mandatory protections of habitual residence prevail over conflicting terms.
