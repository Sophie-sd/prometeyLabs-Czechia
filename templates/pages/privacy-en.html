7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew. To withdraw cookie consent, use "Cookie settings" in the footer. Send requests to info@prometeylabs.com. We reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest. You also have the right to complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ), https://uoou.gov.cz, or to the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

8. Security and incidents

We protect data with reasonable measures (HTTPS, CSRF protection on forms, restricted access). If a serious breach occurs, we notify ÚOOÚ and the affected people without undue delay, as the GDPR requires.

9. Cookies

Details are in our Cookie Policy. We use our own consent banner and Google Consent Mode in basic mode: no Google tag loads before you consent. Google: https://business.safety.google/privacy/.
