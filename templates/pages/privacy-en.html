Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

1. General provisions

1.1

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter — the "Policy") sets out how Individual Entrepreneur Dmytrenko Sofiia Dmytrivna (Tax ID: 3770706565), hereinafter — "We", "Our", "Contractor", collects, processes and protects personal data.

1.2

This Policy applies to any information that users (hereinafter — "You", "User", "Client") provide to us when using the prometeylabs.com website, ordering services, or during any other interaction with us.

1.3

We respect your right to privacy and undertake to protect personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Act No. 110/2019 Coll. on the Processing of Personal Data (Czech Republic).

1.4

Data processing on instruction (Data Processing). When providing development, testing, or technical support services for the Client's web resources, CRM/CMS systems or other software, We may receive temporary access to databases for which the Client is the controller. In such cases, We act exclusively as a Data Processor under Art. 28 GDPR. We process such data strictly in accordance with the Client's documented instructions, do not use it for our own purposes, and undertake to delete or return it after the services have been completed.

2. What data we collect

We collect only the amount of data that is objectively necessary to provide our services and ensure proper communication. In particular:

  • Contact and identification data: first name, last name, phone number, email address, company name, job title (which you provide yourself in contact forms, briefs, or correspondence);
  • Website usage data: IP address, date and time of visit, device type, operating system and browser. This data is collected automatically by servers and cookies;
  • Analytical information: aggregated data from web analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel) on on-site behaviour (pages viewed, session duration, referral sources).

3. Purpose and legal grounds for processing

3.1

We use your data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • concluding and performing IT services agreements (website development, application development, marketing support, etc.);
  • responding to your inquiries and providing customer support;
  • processing financial transactions and preparing accounting documentation;
  • conducting marketing research, scaling and optimising digital advertising campaigns (including via Google Ads), as well as retargeting, subject to your relevant consent given through cookie settings;
  • analysing and improving the functionality and security of our website;
  • complying with applicable legal requirements.

3.2

The legal basis for processing is determined separately for each purpose in accordance with Art. 6(1) GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): concluding and performing IT services agreements; responding to service inquiries; customer support; processing financial transactions and accounting documentation.
  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): marketing research, advertising campaigns (Google Ads) and retargeting — only where you have given the relevant consent via cookie settings (Cookie Banner); on-site behavioural analytics beyond the technically necessary minimum.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): analysing and improving the functionality and security of the website, preventing misuse.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): retaining documents in accordance with applicable Czech and EU accounting and tax legislation (in particular Act No. 563/1991 Coll., on Accounting, and Act No. 235/2004 Coll., on Value Added Tax).

4. Transfer and disclosure of data to third parties

4.1

We do not sell your personal data and do not pass it to advertising agencies for spam mailings.

4.2

Data may be transferred to third parties only to the extent necessary to provide the services, in particular to:

  • payment service providers (Comgate, GoPay and banking institutions) to process transactions;
  • cloud service and hosting providers;
  • web analytics services (in anonymised form).

4.3

Your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside the European Economic Area. Where personal data of EU residents is transferred to countries that have not been recognised by the European Commission as ensuring an adequate level of protection (outside the EEA), We apply appropriate legal safeguards, in particular the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, to ensure the highest security standard.

4.4

Data is disclosed to state or law-enforcement authorities only in cases and in the manner directly provided for by applicable law.

5. Data retention periods

5.1

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes for which it was collected, or for the period required by applicable Czech and EU law (in particular, accounting and tax legislation).

5.2

Web analytics data is retained in accordance with the standard data retention settings of the relevant services (Google, Meta).

6. Your rights

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • know the sources of collection, the location of your personal data, and the purpose of its processing;
  • obtain access to your personal data;
  • request the rectification, update or erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten");
  • withdraw your consent to data processing at any time;
  • lodge complaints regarding data processing with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ) or with a court;
  • request data portability in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • request restriction of processing of your data;
  • object to the processing of your data for direct marketing or profiling purposes;
  • lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.

7. Data security and limitation of liability

7.1

We apply up-to-date technical and organisational measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss or disclosure (including HTTPS encryption and access control for databases).

7.2

Limitation of liability: We take all commercially reasonable and technical security measures to protect your data. However, you understand that transmitting information over the internet carries inherent risks. The Contractor is liable for a breach of personal data security solely to the extent of its proven fault and in accordance with applicable law.

7.3

Data breach notification: In the event of unauthorised access, loss or disclosure of personal data that may pose a high risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects, We undertake to notify the relevant supervisory authorities (no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of it) and the affected users directly, in the manner prescribed by Czech law and the GDPR.

8. Use of cookies

Our website uses cookies to ensure the correct operation of the interface and to collect analytics. Detailed information about which cookies we use and how you can manage them is provided in our separate Cookie Policy.

9. Contact information

9.1

The person responsible for data protection matters is Individual Entrepreneur Dmytrenko Sofiia Dmytrivna (Tax ID: 3770706565). Under Art. 37 GDPR, an individual entrepreneur of this scale of activity is not required to appoint a separate Data Protection Officer (DPO) — the function of the data protection contact person is performed by the Contractor personally.

9.2

If you have any questions about this Policy or wish to exercise your rights regarding personal data, please contact us by email: info@prometeylabs.com.

10. Changes to the Policy

We reserve the right to periodically update this Policy. Changes take effect upon publication of the new version on this page (with the "Last updated" date indicated at the top of the document). Continued use of the website after changes have been made constitutes your agreement to the updated terms.
