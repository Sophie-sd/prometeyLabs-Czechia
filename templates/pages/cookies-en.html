This policy covers cookies and similar technologies (e.g. sessionStorage) on https://prometeylabs.cz. 2. Legal basis

  • We set necessary cookies without consent because we cannot provide the service you asked for without them (Section 89(3) of Czech Act No. 127/2005 Coll.).
  • We set analytics and marketing cookies only with your prior consent (Section 89(3) of Act No. 127/2005 Coll.; Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • If you make no choice, we do not treat that as consent. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.

3. Consent banner

We manage consent with our own first-party banner (no third-party service). On your first visit it offers "Accept all", "Reject all" (both buttons equally prominent) and settings for each category.

  • We store your choice in the pl_consent cookie: for 12 months if you accept and 6 months if you reject. After that we ask you again.
  • We log each choice on our server (/consent/log/) with a random consent ID, the choice and the time, so that we can prove consent (Art. 7(1) GDPR). Log retention: 3 years, after which the record is deleted automatically.
  • You can change or withdraw consent at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent.

5. Google Consent Mode (basic)

We use Google Consent Mode in basic mode: no Google tag loads until you consent, and nothing is sent to Google before then.

  • Analytics tags run only after you consent to Analytics ( analytics_storage ).
  • Google Ads tags run only after you consent to Marketing, which grants both ad_storage and ad_user_data.

7. Attributing enquiries to campaigns

If you arrive from an ad, the link may contain the Google click ID (gclid) and utm_* parameters. We save them in your browser's sessionStorage only if you consent to Marketing. Without consent we keep them only in the memory of the open page; they disappear when you go to another page or close the tab.

When you submit an enquiry form, we send the gclid and utm_* together with your enquiry to our KeyCRM system, even without marketing consent. We do this on the basis of our legitimate interest in handling the enquiry and knowing which campaign it came from (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object to this processing at info@prometeylabs.com.

9. Browser settings

You can delete or block cookies in your browser. If you block necessary cookies, forms may stop working. The easiest way to manage analytics and marketing cookies is "Cookie settings" in the footer.

10. Changes and contact

We post changes on this page with a new date. Contact: info@prometeylabs.com. Czech supervisory authority: Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ), https://uoou.gov.cz.
